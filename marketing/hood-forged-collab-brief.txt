Hood Forged — Collaboration Brief

Hood Forged is a formula-based NFT project. Users enter a math formula and the app forges a unique pixel character from it. The same formula always produces the same character. Each character has a Power stat, and holders can stake their NFT as a worker to gather new formulas and earn $UNVX over time. The project is still early and building its community.
